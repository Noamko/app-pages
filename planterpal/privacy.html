PlanterPal

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

PlanterPal doesn't collect any data.

Everything you put into PlanterPal, like the plants you add, their rooms, schedules and notes, and when you watered them, stays on your iPhone. It's part of your iPhone's own backups, but the app never sends it to us or to anyone else.

No tracking

PlanterPal has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't share anything with third parties.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes everything it stored.

Children

PlanterPal doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be on this page, with a new date at the top.
